# MESSCUT glossary

- **Direction** — one of two service groups on the services hub: Branding or Marketing.
- **Service** — a non-public CPT entry. The hub is `/poslugy/`. Old `/services/{slug}/` URLs redirect to `/poslugy/#svc-{slug}`.
- **Locked service** — Strategy implementation. It is shown, and it cannot be booked until brand strategy exists.
- **Case document** — the chapter JSON for a case study (hero, results, tasks, process, and the later chapters).
- **Lead** — a private submission from the two-step form (email, Telegram, or WhatsApp).
